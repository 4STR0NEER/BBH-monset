# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + Tailwind v4, Motion for UI motion. This matches the studio's other client builds (Wander Cafe, JazzRoc), so the team already knows how to maintain it. Auth and data go through one mock `api.js` seam (localStorage) that Supabase can replace later. Repo: github.com/4STR0NEER/BBH-monset.

## Users

- **Cebu business owners and founders** (SMEs, family businesses, startups across Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu and Talisay). They found the group on Facebook and want to know whether the breakfasts are worth an early morning. Their job is to understand what Business Breakfast Hub is, see when the next breakfast is, and sign up.
- **Members** who come back to the site to check the schedule, read community business news, and see which members are featured.
- **BBH organisers**, who want the site to make the community look serious and bring in more members.

## Product Purpose

Business Breakfast Hub (BBH) is a Cebu business community that meets over breakfast. The site turns Facebook interest into registered members. It also gives members a business board with news, the breakfast schedule and member features. Success means more Cebu owners register, and members come back to the board.

Monset is building it pro bono. The site is also Monset's showcase to the business owners in this community, so the craft has to stand out.

## Positioning

The breakfast table is the product. A member is someone with a seat at the table. Registration means reserving that seat: you fill in a place card that turns into your member card. No other Cebu business group's site can truthfully copy that ritual.

## Operating Context

- Most visitors arrive on mobile from the Facebook page (facebook.com/adminBBH, titled "Bizbreak Hub" there).
- Breakfasts happen early in the morning, in person, in Cebu.
- Reference vibe: pef.co (spare, declarative, members only) and cub.club. Use them as references for tone, not as templates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public landing page that explains BBH and leads to registration.
- Registration and sign in through the "reserve a seat" flow. For now this is mock auth stored in the browser.
- Member business board: business news, breakfast schedule, member features and similar sections.
- All data is dummy data for now, labelled as sample.
- Name: "Business Breakfast Hub", with "BBH" as the short form.

## Brand Commitments

- Cebu identity is required. The brand should feel rooted in Cebu, not generic Manila or Silicon Valley.
- BBH's own palette is yellow ochre and black. The user confirmed this on 2026-11-02 and it's binding.
- Logo: "BBH" set in a condensed sans serif inside a rounded square drawn in orange or ochre line. No logo file has been supplied yet; the site recreates it.
- Feel: sophisticated and casual. The user rejected the first, enamelware build as too cartoony and pop art.
- The hero table uses a real photo with interactive seat markers (user's choice). The current photo is a stand-in from Unsplash until BBH has its own.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There is no member count, testimonials, venue, founder names, dates or photos. Never invent these as facts. Dummy content must be clearly sample and must not be presented as real BBH claims.

## Product Principles

1. The table comes before the pitch. Show the breakfast and the people, not abstract "networking".
2. Make signing up feel like being welcomed, not like filling in a form.
3. Keep Cebu specific: real places, Cebuano warmth and local business reality.
4. Keep it honest: no invented numbers or endorsements.
5. Mobile first, because the audience comes from Facebook.
